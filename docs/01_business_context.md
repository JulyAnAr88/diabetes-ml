# 01_business_context.md

## Problem

A healthcare organization wants to identify patients at risk of diabetes early.

Goal:

* Predict which patients are likely to have diabetes
* Identify key risk factors for diabetes
* Support early intervention and preventive care strategies

---

## Business Questions

* What is the overall diabetes prevalence in the dataset?
* Which patient profiles are most associated with diabetes?
* Which health indicators are the strongest predictors of diabetes?
* Can we identify high-risk patients early for preventive intervention?

---

## Output Expectations

* Actionable insights (not just statistics)
* Clear patient segments with higher diabetes risk
* Interpretable model outputs
* Business-friendly reporting for healthcare decision-makers

---

## Important Notes

* Correlation is not causation
* Avoid overclaiming insights
* Focus on explainability
* Model predictions should support, not replace, clinical judgment
